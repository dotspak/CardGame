# 🃏 Pecto Beta 2.0.1 Patch Notes — The Assistance Update 🃏

## Overview

The main goal of this update was to further refine the new resource system by properly supporting it with other aspects of the game. The main way this was achieved was through the new combat sytem as well as changes to activtable cards.

## System Updates

### Combat Overhaul

- When you declare an attack with a card you control, you can target cards in directly in front or to the left or right columns of your card.
- To damage the player directly, you must have all zones in the card's targetting area open.
- You can no longer attack the opponent's back row.
- After you declare your first attack for the turn, you can no longer play cards from your hand.
  - You may still activate card effects.

### Activation Updates

Skills

- Skills can be activated if the card is ready.
- Activating a skill expends the card.

Reusables

- Reusables can be activated if the card is ready.
- Activating a reusable does not expend the card.

BUFFED: Spells

- Spells can be activated at any time, even if the card is used.
- Activating a spell sends the card from the discard from play.

NEW: Assists

- Assists can ONLY be activated when the card is being expended to play another card.

Along with these changes, skill names have been removed.
